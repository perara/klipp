# Code of conduct

Klipp follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
In short: be kind and constructive, assume good intent, and leave harassment, insults and
personal attacks out of every issue, pull request and discussion.

The maintainer ([@perara](https://github.com/perara)) reviews and acts on reports. To report
something privately, use GitHub's
[private reporting](https://github.com/perara/klipp/security/advisories/new) and say it's about
conduct. Reports are handled confidentially, and the consequences follow the Contributor
Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
